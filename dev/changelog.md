| 2026-07-27 | **Phoneme label split + IPA + phoneme-coverage correctness** | Three user-reported items, plus the coupled correctness work each one forced, plus a fourth defect the investigation turned up. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-27-phoneme-labels-ipa-oov-design.md`. Branch `feat/phoneme-labels-ipa-oov`. Prefs **v19→v20 (removal-migration)**. <br><br>**(1) The fused phoneme label was split. BREAKING.** `phoneme/<stim>_phonemes.csv` packed the ARPABET segment and its stress digit into one `Label` string (`EY2`), unanalysable without string surgery. `Label` is **replaced** (not kept alongside — it is fully derivable) by four columns: `ARPABET` (bare code, `EY`), `Stress` (**nullable `Int64`** — `0`/`1`/`2` on vowels, **empty** on consonants), `IPA`, and `Pron_Source`. The nullable dtype is the point, not an implementation detail: empty means "stress does not apply to this segment" while `0` means "unstressed vowel", and a plain `int64` column physically cannot hold both — collapsing them turns every consonant in the corpus into an unstressed vowel. The **syllable** CSV's `Label`/`ARPABET` were deliberately left alone (`ARPABET` still carries stress digits, `D-R-IY0`), so the two levels now encode stress differently on purpose; the user chose additive-only there to avoid breaking existing consumers of that string. <br><br>**(2) IPA added.** New `extractors/arpabet_ipa.py` — pure, dependency-free, no I/O: `ARPABET_TO_IPA` (39 phones), `to_ipa(arpabet, stress)`, `syllable_to_ipa(pairs)`. New `IPA` column on the phoneme CSV (one segment) and a dash-joined `IPA` on the syllable CSV (`d-ɹ-i`), the latter opt-in via `feat_syllable_ipa` (default off, matching `feat_syllable_arpabet`). **`IPA` is not a pure function of `ARPABET`** — it reads `Stress` too, because two ARPABET vowels are two distinct IPA vowels depending on stress: `AH0`→`ə` (schwa, English's commonest vowel) vs `AH1`/`AH2`→`ʌ`, and `ER0`→`ɚ` vs `ER1`/`ER2`→`ɝ`. No `ˈ`/`ˌ` stress marks are emitted — the `Stress` column carries that, and these are segment-level rows with no correct place to put a syllable-level diacritic. `spn` and any unrecognised code map to `""`, never a guess. <br><br>**(3a) Investigation: out-of-dictionary words were silently deleted.** The reported symptom "some words had no phonemes" was real and had **two independent causes**, both reproduced against the ten real stimuli in `validation/`. First cause: MFA aligns any word absent from `english_us_arpa.dict` using its `<unk>` model, whose single phone is `spn`; `utils/textgrid_parser.py` filtered `spn` as silence, so the word reached `syllabify_pairs` with an **empty phone list** and `syllabify.py` skipped it — **zero phoneme rows and zero syllable rows, no warning**. Reproduced by re-aligning `validation/data/audio/1_M.wav` with `Bella` replaced by the nonsense word `Zorblax`: <br><code>words tier:&nbsp; ('zorblax', 0.00, 0.33)&nbsp; ← the word IS aligned</code><br><code>phones tier: ('spn',&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;0.00, 0.33)&nbsp; ← filtered out as silence</code><br><code>result:&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; WORD WITH ZERO PHONES: zorblax</code><br>`spn` means **out-of-dictionary**, not *not spoken*. Any stimulus with unusual proper nouns lost those words from every sub-word output. The ten validation stimuli happen not to trigger it (`Adrianna` is in-dictionary), which is why it went unnoticed for two months. **Fix:** `parse_phones` keeps `spn` (silence set is now `{'', 'sil', 'SIL', 'sp'}`), so the word yields exactly one syllable + one phoneme row with `ARPABET='spn'`, empty `Stress`/`IPA`, `Pron_Source='oov_unresolved'`, and its real MFA timing. `Phone.is_vowel` is already `False` for `spn` (no trailing digit), so `syllabify_word` takes its existing vowel-less branch — the `hmm` path, no new branch. `add_phoneme_surprisal` emits **`NaN`** for `spn` rows rather than the OOV cap: the cap means "a real phoneme sequence walked off the lexicon", `NaN` means "there is no phoneme identity to score at all". Belt-and-braces: `syllabify_pairs` now collects zero-phoneme words and reports them (`[phonemes] WARNING: 2 words had no phonemes (likely OOV): zorblax, mciver`), excluding punctuation and shared-alignment tokens whose absence is expected. <br><br>**(3b) Investigation: contractions and possessives duplicated every phoneme.** Second cause. spaCy splits `wasn't` into `was` + `n't`; MFA has one word `wasn't`. The *join* repair rule in `extractors/align_merge.py` assigns the **same** `[Onset, End]` to both token rows, and `_phones_for_word` selects by interval containment — so **each token received the complete phone set**, the same acoustic phonemes emitted twice with identical timestamps. Confirmed in `3_M` (12 rows for `was`+`n't`) and `LJ003-0007` (10 rows for `debtor`+`'s`). **Fix:** `syllabify_pairs` tracks emitted `(doc_id, Onset, End)` intervals (rounded to 6 dp) and emits rows only for the **first** token claiming an interval. The word CSV's existing `Transcript` column already shows both tokens aligned to `wasn't`, so the second token's absence is self-explanatory and needed no new column. Measured effect on real stimuli: `3_M` phonemes **111 → 105** (−6) and syllables **41 → 39** (−2); `LJ003-0007` phonemes **124 → 119** (−5) and syllables **48 → 46** (−2); the other eight stimuli unchanged. Note the removed count is *half* the flagged rows (12 flagged in `3_M` = 6 kept + 6 removed). **Rule-based clitic splitting was considered and rejected** (`n't`→`N T`, `'s`→`Z`, …): it needs a hand-maintained table that mis-splits silently on edge cases, and in `wasn't` the `AH0` belongs cleanly to neither token. <br><br>**(3c) The side effect that made 3b a `.mat` data bug, not just a row-count bug.** `phoneme_surprisal_mat_vars` rasterizes scores onto the analysis grid with `surp[idx] += …`, i.e. **same-sample collisions sum**. The duplicated contraction rows landed on identical sample indices, so the `phoneme_surprisal` and `cohort_entropy` impulse trains in `phoneme/<stim>_phoneme_surprisal.mat` carried **doubled** values at every contraction, and `word_onset` fired twice at one instant. Nothing raised. Deduplicating the rows fixed the `.mat` output as a side effect — archived `.mat` sidecars from before this batch are affected wherever the stimulus contains contractions or possessives and should be regenerated. <br><br>**(3d) Optional G2P recovery for OOV words, default OFF.** `aligner._run_mfa` passes `--g2p_model_path mfa_data/pretrained_models/g2p/english_us_arpa.zip` when the new `alignment.g2p_oov` pref is on **and** the model file exists (otherwise it warns and proceeds). MFA then generates a pronunciation from the spelling and aligns it with real boundaries. Verified on the `Zorblax` corpus: `zorblax` received `Z AO1 R B L AE2 K S`, zero words were left phone-less, and — importantly — **in-dictionary phones were untouched**: diffing the two phone tiers, the only change outside the OOV word was its shared boundary with the next word moving 0.33 → 0.35 s; every other interval was bit-identical. Cache invalidation: `_freshness_dict` gains `mfa_g2p` and `SIDECAR_VERSION` goes 1→2, so `_is_fresh` re-aligns existing TextGrids exactly once and toggling the pref re-aligns thereafter. `g2p` is **keyword-only with default `False`** on every touched aligner function (`align_with_phones`, `ensure_alignments`, `_write_sidecar`, `_is_fresh`, `_run_mfa`) because every pre-existing call site passes positionally — a positional parameter would have broken them all at once. `utils/download_models.py` fetches the g2p model (~11 MB into the already-vendored, git-ignored `mfa_data/`). It defaults off deliberately: the pronunciation is **modeled from spelling, not observed**, so no run silently gains hypothesised phone identities. **Known gap, documented not fixed:** `align_with_phones` (the per-document path) checks only that `<stem>.TextGrid` *exists* under `persist_dir` and never consults the freshness sidecar, so a stale non-G2P TextGrid is trusted and flipping the option changes nothing on that path. The **batch** path (`ensure_alignments`, which both `run_word_level_batch` and `run_doc_level_batch` call via `_prealign_batch`) is safe — its sidecar records `mfa_g2p` and forces a re-align. Documented in `docs/features/g2p_oov.md` with the workaround (delete the TextGrid + `.json`, or use the batch path). <br><br>**(3e) `Pron_Source` provenance derivation.** MFA does not mark G2P-derived words in the TextGrid, so provenance is inferred: word in dictionary → `dictionary`; not in dictionary with real phones → `g2p`; not in dictionary with only `spn` → `oov_unresolved`. The form checked is the **MFA words-tier form** (the word frame's `Transcript`), **never the spaCy token** — this is load-bearing because `transcript_prep` spells numbers out before MFA, so spaCy holds `48` while MFA's word is `forty eight`; judging by the token would mark **every number in the corpus** as a modeled pronunciation (verified: `48`, `2`, `6`, `8`, `10`, `3` are all absent from the dictionary as digit strings and all present as their spelled forms). A `Transcript` holding several space-separated words (the `align_merge` *expand* case) counts as `dictionary` only when **every** part is in the lexicon. The lexicon key set is memoized in `syllabify._lexicon_words()`, reusing the same `english_us_arpa.dict` that `phoneme_surprisal.load_mfa_lexicon` already parses rather than adding a second read of a 200k-line file; an unreadable dictionary reports `dictionary` rather than mislabelling the whole corpus `g2p`. <br><br>**(4) Whitespace tokens no longer occupy word-CSV rows.** Found during the investigation. spaCy emits a literal newline as a token (`upos == 'SPACE'`, text `\n`), and `text_parser.parse` iterated every token, so each hard line wrap became an **all-NaN word-CSV row consuming both a `token_id` and a `token_in_sentence_id`** — every word after a line break was off by one in both columns. Affected 3 of the 10 validation stimuli (the hard-wrapped `LJ*` files); the single-line ISC stimuli have no SPACE tokens. `master_plan.md` §4's Brain Treebank parity claim ("`token_in_sentence_id` gives `Index_in_Sentence`") was therefore **false** for newline-containing transcripts; §4 has been corrected. **Fix:** skip `token.is_space`; assign `token_id` over surviving tokens only; and — **mandatory, not optional** — build a `{token.i → token_id}` map and resolve `head_token_id` **through it**. The old code computed `head_token_id = token.head.i + 1` from spaCy's doc index, which counts SPACE tokens; that agreed with `token_id` only *by accident* because `token_id` counted them too, so skipping the rows without the remap would have pointed every `head_token_id` past the first newline at the wrong word with no error raised. Verified on the real transcripts before implementing: LJ002-0321 (26 rows, 3 space tokens skipped), LJ003-0007 (35 rows, 2 skipped) and a synthetic blank-line case — ids contiguous, heads resolve, roots self-headed in all three; and no token's spaCy head is ever a whitespace token in any of them, so the map's `.get(..., self)` fallback is defensive only. **Nothing was lost:** these newlines are hard line *wraps*, not paragraph breaks — in `LJ002-0321` one falls mid-sentence (`'…time of distribution\nwere often 48 hours…'`, one sentence, both halves `sentence_id=1`), so a paragraph counter incremented per `\n` would split a sentence in two. Paragraphs are already defined by **blank** lines: `text_parser` has computed `paragraph_id` from `\n[ \t]*\n+` all along, with a pref, a column-drop entry, a GUI toggle, planner `emits_columns`, a doc, and doc-level `n_paragraphs`. So no new column, pref, toggle, migration entry or doc was needed for paragraphs — an earlier draft of the spec was wrong about this and was corrected. A sentence-level `paragraph_id` was **not** added (out of scope; derivable by joining the word CSV on `sentence_id`). <br><br>**The two coupled changes that would have failed silently.** Both read the stress digit out of the `Label` string and neither raises: **(i)** `extractors/pcenter.py` detected the vowel nucleus via `group['Label'].str[-1:].str.isdigit()` → now `Stress.notna()`. Had this been missed, **no vowel would ever be detected** and every `P_Center` would have quietly fallen back to the syllable onset. `P_Center` bit-identity is consequently the #1 real-stimulus validation check. **(ii)** `extractors/phoneme_surprisal.py` read `Label` → now reads the already-stress-free `ARPABET`, rebuilding the fused form from `ARPABET + Stress` for the `strip_stress=False` path. This one would at least have raised `KeyError`. <br><br>**Out of scope, explicitly: "was this word actually spoken?"** MFA is a *forced* aligner — it assumes the transcript is what was said and must place every word somewhere, so a word present in the transcript but absent from the audio gets squeezed into a sliver, stealing time from its neighbours. That is **already true today for every in-dictionary word** and is neither introduced nor fixed here. G2P does not detect absence either: verified, because `1_M.wav` actually says "Bella", the G2P run confidently aligned `Z AO1 R B L AE2 K S` across the interval where "Bella" was spoken. G2P recovers pronunciations for *unusual words that were spoken*; it does not adjudicate whether they were. Detecting genuine transcript/audio mismatch needs per-word alignment likelihood, and MFA 3.4.0's `align` CLI exposes no such output (checked: no likelihood/confidence flag, no `alignment_analysis.csv` produced). That stays parked as the roadmap's *Correctness scoring* item — and is why G2P defaults off. <br><br>**Prefs v19→v20 (removal-migration**, precedent v14 struct-id rename and v18 predictability unification, handled in the same `_merged_from_user` finalize block**).** Removed: `feat_phoneme_label`. Added: `feat_phoneme_arpabet` / `feat_phoneme_stress` / `feat_phoneme_ipa` / `feat_phoneme_pron_source`, each **inheriting the removed key's stored value** so a user who had the label column off keeps all four off; `feat_syllable_ipa` (`False`); and a new top-level **`alignment`** section holding `g2p_oov` (`False`). The `alignment` section needed no `config_yaml` routing change — `_RUN_SECTIONS` is `("shared",)` and everything else lands in `features.yaml`, which is semantically right (a "what to extract" setting) and means the GUI's "Reset settings" turns `g2p_oov` back off, correct for an opt-in. `_SECTION_COMMENTS` gained a line for it. `scripts/export_default_config.py` re-run; the drift-guard test enforces it. <br><br>**Coupled layers touched:** `extractors/arpabet_ipa.py` (new), `syllabify.py` (`_PHON_COLUMNS`/`_SYLL_COLUMNS`, split columns, dedup, zero-phoneme reporting, `_lexicon_words`/`_pron_source`), `pcenter.py`, `phoneme_surprisal.py`, `text_parser.py`, `aligner.py`, `utils/textgrid_parser.py`, `utils/download_models.py`, `utils/preferences.py`, `utils/config_yaml.py`, `main.py` (`_PHONEME_COLUMN_MAP` +4/−1, `_SYLLABLE_COLUMN_MAP` +`feat_syllable_ipa`, `--g2p-oov`, pref threaded to `_prealign_batch`), `gui/planner/registry.py` + `emit.py`, `gui/section_subword_features.py` (one phoneme-label toggle → four, syllable `IPA` row, G2P card), `run.py`, `gui/feature_docs.py`. `utils/session_units.py` correctly needed **no** change (`_STATIC_UNITS` maps only physical-unit columns; the new label columns are categorical). `utils/doc_summary.py` needed no change (it references no syllable or phoneme columns). <br><br>**Docs:** `docs/features/phoneme.md` rewritten (new column table; the empty-vs-`0` `Stress` trap; the three `Pron_Source` values and why `oov_unresolved` ≠ "unspoken"; why a token can legitimately have no phoneme rows). New `docs/features/feat_phoneme_ipa.md` (full 39-phone table with dictionary-verified example words, the stress-dependent `AH`/`ER` rows, why no `ˈ`/`ˌ`, why `IPA` depends on `Stress`) and `docs/features/g2p_oov.md` (what G2P is, modeled-not-observed, defaults off, re-aligns once, does **not** detect whether a word was spoken, and the per-document staleness gap).
