6.5Acoustic level

Audio-only time series in acoustic/<stim>_acoustic.mat, on one shared time grid set by the analysis rate.

6.5.1Time series

App: Acoustic features page → Time series card.

6.5.1 Amplitude envelope (broadband)
The broadband amplitude envelope is the slow-varying &quot;loudness contour&quot; of the audio — how the overall energy of the signal rises and falls over time, with the fast carrier (the individual pressure oscillations) stripped away.
6.5.2 Onset envelope (HWR derivative)
The onset envelope marks where the acoustic energy is rising — the moments the signal gets suddenly louder.
6.5.3 Log-mel spectrogram
The log-mel spectrogram is a time × frequency picture of the audio in which the frequency axis is warped to the perceptual mel scale (fine resolution at low frequencies, coarse at high) and the magnitude is in decibels.
6.5.4 MFCC (Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients)
Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs) are a compact, decorrelated summary of the spectral shape at each instant.
